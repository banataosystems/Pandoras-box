# Prioritizing Roadmap by Evidence

## Outcome

A ranked roadmap that favors evidence-producing work and records explicit deferrals.

## Use when

- A major feature or market is proposed.
- The backlog is growing faster than evidence.
- The team must choose between product, infrastructure, or validation work.

## Workflow

1. Identify the paying user and problem for each candidate.
2. Assess pain, frequency, outcome impact, retention, revenue, defensibility, COGS, implementation cost, and new risk.
3. Determine whether the item is needed now, later, or not at all.
4. Define the smallest safe experiment and evidence threshold.
5. Record the opportunity cost and what is deferred.

## Proof required

- Decision matrix with sources.
- Experiment and kill criteria.
- Dependency-aware ranking and deferrals.

## Stop conditions

- The proposal has no identifiable customer problem or payer.
- A high-risk feature lacks a fail-closed experiment.
- The ranking depends on invented performance data.

## Outputs

- `evidence-ranked-roadmap`
- `feature-decision`
- `deferral-register`
